General Information
  • This is a Computer Based Entry Test (CBNET / NET) conducted by NUST Islamabad.
  • The test consists of 200 MCQs with a total duration of 180 minutes.
  • Each correct answer = 1 mark. No negative marking.
  • The test is divided into sections based on your selected field.
Test Structure by Field
  • NET-Engineering: Mathematics (100) + Physics (60) + English (40)
  • NET-Applied Sciences: Biology (100) + Chemistry (60) + English (40)
  • NET-Business & Social Sciences: Quantitative Maths (100) + English (100)
  • NET-Architecture: Design Aptitude (100) + Mathematics (60) + English (40)
  • NET-Natural Sciences: Mathematics (100) + English (100)
How to Navigate
  • Click on any option to select your answer.
  • Press Save to record your answer and advance.
  • Use Next / Prev to navigate.
  • Click numbers in the Navigation Grid to jump.
Colour Legend
  • Grey = Not answered
  • Green = Saved
  • Yellow = Marked for review
  • Red Border = Active question
Aggregate Formula: 75% NET + 15% FSc/A-Level + 10% Matric/O-Level. Use the Aggregate Calculator next.
Important: Do not refresh or close the browser. Unfair means will lead to cancellation.
